Dr. Mohamed Kamel Elshaarawy

Civil Engineer | Assistant Lecturer | Ph.D. Candidate | Researcher

Mohamed Kamel Elshaarawy is an Assistant Lecturer of Civil Engineering at Horus University–Egypt (HUE) and a Ph.D. candidate in Irrigation and Hydraulics Engineering at Tanta University. He holds a B.Sc. (2018, Excellent with Honors) and M.Sc. (2024) from Port Said University, specializing in hydraulics, water resources management, and structural design.

His research applies machine learning, optimization, and computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to seepage control, saltwater intrusion, hydraulic structures, groundwater systems, and concrete performance. He has authored 35+ peer-reviewed publications, serves as an active reviewer for international journals, and contributes actively to teaching and academic administration at HUE.
