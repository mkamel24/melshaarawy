Civil Engineer • Assistant Lecturer • Ph.D. Candidate • Researcher

I am an Assistant Lecturer of Civil Engineering at Horus University–Egypt (HUE) and a Ph.D. candidate in Irrigation & Hydraulics Engineering at Tanta University. My research connects hydraulics, CFD, optimization, and machine learning to solve real problems in seepage control, saltwater intrusion, groundwater systems, hydraulic structures, and concrete performance.
