About

Short bio

Mohamed Kamel Elshaarawy is an Assistant Lecturer of Civil Engineering at Horus University-Egypt (HUE) and a Ph.D. candidate in Irrigation and Hydraulics Engineering at Tanta University. He holds B.Sc. (2018, Excellent with Honors) and M.Sc. (2024) degrees from Port Said University. His research applies machine learning, optimization, and computational modeling to seepage control, groundwater systems, hydraulic structures, and concrete performance.
